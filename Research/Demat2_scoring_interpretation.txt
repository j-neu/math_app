Scoring & Interpretation (DEMAT 2+)
Scoring Method
Step 1: Raw Score Calculation

Use transparent answer templates (Auswertungsschablonen) overlaid on test pages
Count correct answers in each of the 10 subtests
Addition and Subtraction scored separately
Sum all subtests → Total Raw Score (Rohwert ges)
Interpretation Method
Norm-Referenced Comparison:
The raw score is compared against norm tables based on a sample of 4,014 German children (2,298 in grade 2; 1,716 in grade 3), separated by grade level AND gender.

Two Key Metrics:

Percentile Ranks (PR) — shows what % of the norm group scored at or below the child's level

96-100: Outstanding (ausgezeichnet)
76-95: Above average (überdurchschnittlich)
26-75: Average (durchschnittlich)
11-25: Below average (unterdurchschnittlich)
6-10: Poor (schwach)
1-5: Very poor (sehr schwach)
T-Values — standardized scores where 50 = mean, with 68% confidence bands built in

Used primarily for dyscalculia diagnosis
Accounts for measurement error
Diagnosis of Dyscalculia (Rechenschwäche)
Key Criterion:

A 12–15 T-value point difference between math performance and IQ
The DEMAT 2+ provides T-values for every possible raw score to enable this comparison
Identifies specific learning disability vs. general low achievement
Result Profile (Ergebnisprofil)
Graphical display of subtest-by-subtest performance
Important caveat: Subtest scores are for initial screening only, not diagnostic
Used to identify possible problem areas for further individual assessment
Tom example (2nd grade):
Overall: 24 points → T=53 → 55th percentile (average)
Weaknesses: doubling (PR=19) and halving (PR=10) tasks
Conclusion: targeted support in these areas, no comprehensive intervention needed
Key Results from Standardization
Significant gender differences: boys scored ~3 points higher (statistically significant but small effect size)
Two test forms (A/B) showed no differences → results are comparable
Normal distribution across all student groups
